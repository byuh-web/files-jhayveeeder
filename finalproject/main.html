Welcome to the Pokémon Trading Card Game Tutorial!

The purpose of this site is to introduce the Pokémon Trading Card Game (TCG), learn more about the kinds of Pokémon Cards and to know the rules on how to play it.

The Pokémon TCG is a fun and exciting game where players use Pokémon cards, Energy cards, and Trainer cards to battle against each other. By learning the different card types and basic rules, you can begin building your own deck and developing strategies to win battles. This website will guide you through the fundamentals of the game and help you become a confident Pokémon Trainer. If you would like to learn more about the Pokémon TCG and its history, feel free to visit the Pokémon Trading Card Game article on Wikipedia.
