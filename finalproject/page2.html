Why is this important?

Just like the ones in the Pokémon video games, a Pokémon's strength does not only depend on the Pokémon itself. As a trainer, you will need to use different kinds of items such as Potions & TMs to make your Pokémon stronger This concept is also followed in the Pokémon TCG. While Pokémon cards are essential t the game, you will also need to use other types of Pokémon cards to battle and win the game. Using the right cards can give you some advantages. Each type of card has an important role in helping you to build a strong deck and win against your opponent. Understanding the different types of Pokémon cards is the first step toward mastering the Pokémon TCG.
