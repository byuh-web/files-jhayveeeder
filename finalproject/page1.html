In order for you to start playing the Pokémon TCG, you must have your own Pokémon cards first. You can either buy booster packs and trainer boxes for a chance to get a rare card, or if you want to get the card you really wanted, you can also buy individual cards from card shops or online marketplaces. Once you have collected enough cards, you can build a deck that matches your preferred playstyle and begin your battle against other Trainers.

You can also buy Pokémon cards at the comfort of your home. You can either buy directly from the Pokémon website at MSRP, or you can buy though reputable resellers such as Walmart and Target for an additional price.

If you prefer single cards, websites such as Ebay and TCGPlayer are a great place to start.
