Why this topic?

I chose Favorite Quotes from the Apostles because, as a member of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, I believe in giving heed to the words of the leaders I have sustained. Their teachings help me better understand the gospel and guide my daily choices. I find strength and direction in their counsel as I try to follow Jesus Christ.

Elder David A. Bednar

“To be offended is a choice we make; it is
not a condition inflicted or imposed upon us
by someone or something else.”

- Ensign, 2006

Elder Patrick Kearon

“God is in relentless pursuit of you. He wants
all of His children to choose to return to Him,
and He employs every possible measure to
bring you back.”

- April 2024 General Conference

Elder Dieter F. Uchtdorf

“My message to all parents is this: The Lord
loves you. He is with you. He stands beside
you. He is your strength in guiding your
children to make righteous choices.”

- April 2023 General Conference

Elder Ronald A. Rasband

“A mission might be the greatest chapter in a
couple’s life. A good title might be ‘My Lord
Will Have Need of Me.’ You may be on
unfamiliar ground; however, the power of the
Spirit will make you feel right at home.”

- October 2023 General Conference

Elder Ulisses F Soares

“Reverence for sacred things is the greatest
manifestation of a vital spiritual quality;
it is a by-product of our connection to
holiness and reflects our love for and
proximity to our Heavenly Father and our
Savior, Jesus Christ. It is also one of the
most elevated experiences of the soul. Such
virtue directs our thoughts, hearts and
lives toward Deity.”

- April 2025 General Conference

Elder Quentin L. Cook

“The sacred scriptures and living prophets
are a major way a loving Heavenly Father
makes His plan of happiness available to all
His children.”

- October 2024 General Conference
