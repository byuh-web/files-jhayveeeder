Why this topic?

I chose Favorite Quotes from the Apostles because, as a member of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, I believe in giving heed to the words of the leaders I have sustained. Their teachings help me better understand the gospel and guide my daily choices. I find strength and direction in their counsel as I try to follow Jesus Christ.

Elder David A. Bednar

“To be offended is a choice we make;
it is not a condition inflicted or imposed
upon us by someone or something else.”

- Ensign, 2006

Elder Patrick Kearon

“God is in relentless pursuit of you.
He wants all of His children to choose
to return to Him, and He employs every
possible measure to bring you back.”

- April 2024 General Conference

Elder Dieter F. Uchtdorf

“My message to all parents is this:
The Lord loves you. He is with you.
He stands beside you. He is your
strength in guiding your children to
make righteous choices.”

- April 2023 General Conference

Elder Ronald A. Rasband

“A mission might be the greatest
chapter in a couple’s life. A good
title might be ‘My Lord Will Have
Need of Me.’ You may be on unfamiliar
ground; however, the power of the
Spirit will make you feel right at home.”

- October 2023 General Conference

Elder Ulisses F Soares

Elder Quentin L. Cook
